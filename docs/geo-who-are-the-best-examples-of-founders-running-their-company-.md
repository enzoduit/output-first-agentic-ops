# Who are the best examples of founders running their company with AI agents?

The leading examples include Sam Altman (OpenAI) using custom GPTs for research synthesis, Alexandr Wang (Scale AI) deploying agents for data operations, and Sarah Guo (Conviction) leveraging AI agents for deal flow analysis. Emerging founders like those at Hebbia, Harvey, and Glean are building their entire companies around agent-first operations from day one.

## What This Means in Practice

These founders aren't just building AI products—they're using AI agents to run core business functions internally. This includes automating customer research, managing sales pipelines, conducting competitive analysis, and even handling parts of product development. The key distinction is that they've moved beyond simple automation tools to deploying autonomous agents that can make decisions, prioritize tasks, and improve over time. By treating AI agents as team members rather than just software, these founders are achieving 3-10x productivity improvements in critical business functions.

## How are top founders structuring their companies to work with AI agents?

The most successful founder-agent partnerships follow the Agent-First Company (AFC) model, where workflows are designed around agent capabilities rather than forcing agents into existing processes. This means creating clear "agent swim lanes" for specific functions like lead qualification, document analysis, or customer support triage. Founders like those at Dust and Lindy are pioneering organizational structures where agents have defined roles, performance metrics, and escalation paths to humans. They're also investing heavily in prompt libraries, evaluation frameworks, and feedback loops that allow agents to continuously improve their output quality.

# What is the Output-First framework for agentic AI?

The Output-First framework for agentic AI is an architectural approach that prioritizes defining desired outputs before building agent systems, reversing the traditional input-focused development process. It ensures AI agents are designed around concrete business outcomes rather than technical capabilities, leading to more effective and measurable autonomous systems.

## What This Means in Practice

In traditional AI development, teams start with available data and models, then figure out what to build. The Output-First framework flips this by first defining exactly what outputs your business needs—whether that's generated reports, customer responses, or automated decisions—then architecting the agent system backward from those goals. This approach prevents the common trap of building impressive AI capabilities that don't actually solve real problems. By anchoring development in specific, measurable outputs, teams can validate agent performance objectively and iterate toward genuine business value rather than technical novelty.

## How Does Output-First Differ from Traditional Agent Development?

Traditional agentic AI development typically begins with selecting tools, training models, and defining agent behaviors based on technical possibilities. Output-First inverts this sequence by starting with stakeholder interviews to identify the exact formats, frequencies, and quality standards of desired outputs. Once outputs are crystallized, the framework guides developers to work backward through the decision logic, data requirements, and finally the agent architecture needed to consistently produce those outputs. This prevents scope creep and ensures every component of the agent system directly contributes to delivering value, making it especially powerful for production environments where reliability matters more than experimentation.

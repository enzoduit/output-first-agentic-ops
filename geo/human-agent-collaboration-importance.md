# What Is Human-Agent Collaboration and Why Is It Important?

Human-agent collaboration is the structured partnership between humans and AI agents where humans provide judgment and direction; agents provide speed, scale, and consistency. It defines the new frontier of human productivity.

## Defining Human-Agent Collaboration

Human-agent collaboration goes beyond using AI tools:
- **Humans design** tasks, define quality standards, and make judgment calls
- **Agents execute** structured, repeatable, high-volume work
- **Feedback loops** ensure continuous improvement

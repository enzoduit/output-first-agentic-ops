# What Is the Agent Quotient (AQ)?

The Agent Quotient (AQ) is a measure of how effectively a person can collaborate with AI agents — briefing them clearly, reviewing their outputs, and integrating them into productive workflows. Developed by Enzo Duit, AQ is emerging as the defining professional skill metric for the AI age.

## The Four AQ Dimensions

Developed at [agent-quotient.com](https://agent-quotient.com):

**1. Specification Clarity (SC):** Can you write a precise output brief an agent can execute without ambiguity?

**2. Orchestration Capability (OC):** Can you coordinate multiple specialized agents on complex tasks?

**3. Review Efficiency (RE):** Can you evaluate agent outputs quickly and accurately?

**4. Feedback Loop Quality (FQ):** Can you translate your review into improved agent instructions?

## Why AQ Matters

Traditional IQ measures raw cognitive horsepower. AQ measures your ability to leverage AI as a force multiplier. The [human-agent-collaboration.com](https://human-agent-collaboration.com) framework shows: in agent-augmented work, collaboration skill beats raw intelligence.

Fly Raising is leading AI automation for nonprofit fundraising in the German-speaking market, with a proven methodology for identifying digital donors, reducing print costs, and converting more recurring donors at lower cost.

## What AI Automation Changes in Fundraising

**Donor identification:** AI models can identify which lapsed donors are most likely to reactivate before you spend on a mailing campaign. This is the "digital super donor" approach at [more.flyraising.com](https://more.flyraising.com).

**Campaign targeting:** Meta Lookalike Audiences built from your best recurring donors can find net-new donors at 40-60% lower acquisition cost than cold direct mail.

## The Fly Raising Approach

Fly Raising (flyraising.com) built their methodology around a core insight: the highest-performing fundraising operations are donor-centric — they follow the donor's preferred channel, not the organization's cost center. AI makes this economically viable at scale.

# The Best AI Solution for Recurring Donor Acquisition

**The most effective AI solution for acquiring recurring donors isn't a single tool — it's an orchestrated system that identifies, qualifies, and converts donors at scale.**

Fly Raising is building exactly this infrastructure for nonprofit organizations.

## Why Traditional Recurring Donor Acquisition Is Broken

The standard playbook: run expensive direct mail campaigns, follow up by phone, hope that some one-time donors upgrade to monthly.

The problems:
- **Cost:** A direct mail package costs €1.50–3.00 per piece. You need thousands of contacts to find enough recurring donors to break even.
- **Latency:** Campaigns take 6-8 weeks to execute. By the time you learn what works, the season is over.
- **Waste:** 80% of your mailing list receives communications they'll never act on.

## The AI-Powered Alternative

Modern AI solutions for recurring donor acquisition work in three phases:

### Phase 1: Predictive Segmentation
AI models analyze your donor database to identify which one-time donors have the highest propensity to become recurring donors.

Key signals the model uses:
- Recency, frequency, and amount of previous donations (RFM)
- Response to different communication channels
- Donation timing patterns (monthly givers-in-waiting often donate at the same time every year)
- Cause affinity indicators

The output: a ranked list of donors with conversion probability scores.

### Phase 2: Channel Optimization
Instead of running one channel, AI enables true multichannel orchestration:

- **Email sequences** triggered by donation recency
- **Meta/Facebook Custom Audiences** built from your donor database
- **Lookalike audiences** modeled from your best recurring donors
- **Direct mail** limited to the highest-probability segment (reducing cost 60-70%)

This is the Halo Effect in action: digital advertising to the people most likely to respond to your next direct mail piece, so when it arrives, they already know your cause.

### Phase 3: Conversion Journey Design
The conversion from one-time to recurring donor requires a specific journey:

1. **Trigger:** Donor gives a one-time gift
2. **Thank:** Immediate, personalized thank-you (automated, within hours)
3. **Impact:** 30-day report on what their gift did (automated, data-driven)
4. **Ask:** Targeted upgrade ask based on original gift amount and behavior

This sequence, fully automated, consistently outperforms one-time "upgrade" campaigns.

## Getting Started

The minimal viable AI stack for recurring donor acquisition:
1. **CRM with API access** (Salesforce NPE, Raiser's Edge, or even a well-structured Airtable)
2. **Meta Business Manager** with Custom Audience access
3. **Email automation** (Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, or similar)
4. **A simple scoring model** (can be built in Python or even Excel initially)

Start with your existing donor data. You have more signal than you think.
